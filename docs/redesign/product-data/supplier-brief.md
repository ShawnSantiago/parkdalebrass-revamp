## 2 · Please quote

1. Unit price per finish (and per size, if it varies), at your minimum order and at the quantities above.
2. Minimum order per design, per finish and per ring size.
3. Tooling, mould or sample charges, and the sample lead time.
4. Production lead time and reorder lead time.
5. How each part is made (cast, die-struck, machined, hand-fabricated).
6. Shipping terms to Toronto (Incoterms), the HS code you'd use, and the country of manufacture, so we can work out duty and landed cost.
7. Packaging included, if any.

## 3 · Documents we need before ordering

For every part (ring; pendant, chain, clasp, jump rings and solder), per finish:

1. **Alloy designation and composition**, with a mill certificate or a lab analysis. If any part is machined from brass rod, tell us the grade (free-machining brass can contain 2.5–3% lead).
2. **Lab reports** from an accredited lab, naming the SKU, finish, batch and date:
   - lead content (ppm) in metal and coatings: target under 0.05% (500 ppm);
   - cadmium content (ppm) in metal parts and solder: target under 0.01% (100 ppm);
   - for the chrome versions, **nickel release to EN 1811**, with EN 12472 wear pre-treatment.
3. **For the chrome plating:** the full plating stack (each layer's metal and thickness in µm). Can you plate chrome without a nickel underlayer, or with a nickel-free one? What do you recommend for a ring that's worn every day?
4. Whether a Brockton ring can be resized without damaging the finish. We plan to offer size exchanges rather than resizing, but we'd like to know.

If you don't have current reports, tell us which lab you'd use and what the tests would cost.

## 4 · Samples

Please quote for one sample of each piece in each finish (the Brockton in sizes [7 and 10]). We'll measure them, check the fit and finish, and photograph them before ordering.
