This policy explains where Parkdale Brass ships, how long delivery takes, what it costs, and what we do if an order is late, lost or damaged. It applies to orders placed at parkdalebrass.com. Returns, exchanges and refunds are covered in full in our Returns and exchanges policy.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in Canada, in every province and territory except Quebec. We don't ship to Quebec or outside Canada at the moment, and checkout accepts only addresses we ship to.

We deliver to the street address you give at checkout. We don't deliver to P.O. boxes.

How long it takes

  • Processing: orders ship 1 to 2 business days after you place them.
  • Delivery: once your order ships, it arrives in 2 to 7 business days, wherever we ship in Canada.

That's 3 to 9 business days from the day you order.

A business day is Monday to Friday, except Ontario public holidays: New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and Boxing Day (December 26). An order placed on a weekend or holiday counts from the next business day.

Shipping costs

  • Orders under $129.00 CAD: $15.00 CAD, flat rate.
  • Orders of $129.00 CAD or more: free.

The $129.00 is the total price of the jewelry in your order, before tax and shipping. The same rate applies everywhere we ship.

Checkout shows the shipping charge, the tax and your total before you pay.

Taxes, duties and currency

Prices on our site are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and don't include tax. Sales tax is added at checkout, and it also applies to the shipping charge.

Your order ships within Canada, so you won't pay customs duties, import taxes or brokerage fees on delivery.

The law also gives you the right to cancel in some cases, for example if your order is late (see below).

If your order is late

You can follow your parcel with the tracking number in your shipping email. If you haven't had a shipping email within 2 business days after you ordered, or your order hasn't arrived within 7 business days after it ships, email us at shawns@parkdalebrass.com and we'll look into it.

If your order hasn't been delivered within 30 days after you placed it, you can cancel it at any time before it's delivered. If you agreed in writing to a later delivery date, the 30 days count from that date instead.

To cancel, tell us you're cancelling. You can tell us any way you like. We suggest a way you can keep a record of, such as an email to shawns@parkdalebrass.com. If you write to us, your notice counts from the day you send it. We refund everything you paid, including shipping, within 15 days after you tell us.

If those 30 days pass and you then agree to take delivery, you can't cancel for late delivery, but your return rights still apply.

If your parcel reaches you after you cancel, send it back within 15 days after you cancel or after you receive it, whichever is later. Send it to our address under "Contact us" below, or to a return address we give you, by a method that confirms delivery, such as a tracked service. It counts as returned on the day you send it. We pay the reasonable cost of sending it back: we can send you a prepaid label, or pay you back what you spent.

If we don't refund you in time and you paid by credit card, you can ask your credit card issuer, in writing, to reverse the charge. You need to ask within 60 days after our 15 days end.

Lost or missing parcels

Your parcel is our responsibility until Canada Post delivers it to your address.

Lost before delivery. If Canada Post loses your parcel, we'll send you a replacement or refund everything you paid for the order, including shipping. You choose. If we can't replace it, for example because the piece is out of stock, we refund it. If you choose a refund, we make it within 15 days after you tell us.

We treat a parcel as lost when Canada Post tells us it is. If your order still hasn't arrived 30 days after you placed it, you can also cancel it, as explained above.

Marked as delivered, but not received. If tracking shows your parcel as delivered but you can't find it:

  1. Check around your door, your mailbox or parcel box, and with neighbours or anyone else at your address.
  2. Check the delivery address in your order confirmation.
  3. If it's still missing, email us at shawns@parkdalebrass.com with your order number, as soon as you can.

We'll ask Canada Post to look into it, and tell you what it finds. If Canada Post finds the parcel was lost, or was delivered to an address other than the one on your order, we treat it as lost, as above.

Damaged in transit

If your jewelry arrives damaged, email us at shawns@parkdalebrass.com within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and photos that show the damage. A photo of the box or mailer helps too, if you still have it.

We'll replace the piece or refund it. You choose. We send you a prepaid label to return the damaged piece, and we ship the replacement to you free. If we can't replace it, for example because it's out of stock, we refund it. Our Returns and exchanges policy explains how refunds are made.

This time limit doesn't shorten your rights under the law. The jewelry you buy must match its description and be of merchantable quality.

Returns and exchanges, in short

  • You can return or exchange unworn pieces in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery. Unworn means you've only tried the piece on to check the fit, and it has no marks, scratches or other signs of wear. Original packaging is the packaging the piece came in, not the outer shipping box or mailer.
  • Each order with a ring includes one free size exchange within those 30 days. We send you a prepaid return label, and we ship the new size to you free. We don't resize rings: if The Brockton doesn't fit, exchange it for another size.
  • If you return an unworn piece for a refund (not an exchange), you pay the return shipping. This doesn't apply when you cancel under a right the law gives you.
  • If a piece arrives damaged, has a fault, or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 30 days of delivery, with photos. You choose a replacement or a refund. We send you a prepaid return label, and a replacement ships to you free.
  • We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days after your return reaches us. Your bank or card provider may take longer to show the refund.

How to start a return or exchange is explained in our Returns and exchanges policy.
