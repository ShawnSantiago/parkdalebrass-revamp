This policy explains how Parkdale Brass collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit parkdalebrass.com, place an order, create an account, subscribe to our emails or contact us.

Personal information is information about an identifiable person, such as your name, email address or delivery address.

At a glance

  • We collect what we need to take and deliver your order, run your account and answer your messages. If you subscribe, we also use your email address to send you our newsletter.
  • We send marketing emails only to people who subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • We use cookies for store reports and marketing only if you accept them in our cookie banner.
  • Shopify runs our store, our checkout and our newsletter. Canada Post delivers our parcels. Shopify also uses some information about your visit for its own purposes, under its own privacy policy.
  • Shopify, and other service providers we use, may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it's there, that country's courts, police and national security authorities may be able to get access to it.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. You can raise a concern with our privacy contact, or with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

What we collect

When you place an order

  • Your name, email address, and shipping and billing addresses, and your phone number if you give one.
  • What you order (the piece, the finish and, for The Brockton, the ring size), the prices, tax and shipping charged, and your order number.
  • Delivery details from Canada Post, such as tracking updates and when your parcel was delivered.
  • Payment details. You enter them in Shopify's checkout, and [payment processor] processes them. We're told whether the payment went through, and we see the details the processor gives us about it, such as the payment method.
  • Information used to screen orders for fraud, such as your IP address and the device and browser you used.

If you return or exchange a piece, we keep a record of your request, what you sent back, and any exchange or refund. If you report a damaged, faulty or wrong piece, this includes the photos you send.

When you create an account

Shopify runs our customer accounts. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code sent to that address, instead of a password. Your account holds your name, email address, saved addresses and order history.

When you subscribe to our emails

We collect your email address and keep a record of your consent, such as when you subscribed.

Subscribing is always your choice, and it's separate from buying. Our sign-up form shows this wording under the email field:

By subscribing, you agree to get emails from Parkdale Brass about new collections and releases. You can unsubscribe at any time. Parkdale Brass, [mailing address], Toronto, ON [postal code] · Contact: shawns@parkdalebrass.com · Privacy policy

No sign-up box is ever ticked for you in advance, on our site or at checkout.

When you contact us

We collect your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message, along with anything else you send us, such as an order number or photos. Messages from our contact form reach us by email.

If you tell us about a safety problem with a piece, such as an injury or a skin reaction, we collect what you tell us about it. This can include information about your health. We use it only to deal with the problem. If the law requires us to report the incident to Health Canada and to our supplier, we give them the information the law requires. We don't give our supplier your name or contact details unless you agree, or the law requires it.

When you browse the site

When you visit, Shopify automatically collects information about your device and your visit. This includes your IP address, your browser and device type, the pages and products you view, what's in your cart, what you search for on the site, and the site that sent you to us. Some of this depends on your cookie choices (see "Cookies and similar technologies").

Information we get from others

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Pixels, tags and scripts are pieces of code that record what happens on a page. Shopify sets the cookies our store uses. They fall into three groups:

  • Needed for the store to work: these keep your cart, run checkout, keep you signed in, remember your cookie choice and protect the site. They're always on, because the store doesn't work without them.
  • Store reports: these tell us how the store is used, such as the pages viewed, the type of device and the site that sent you. Shopify gives us the results as reports.
  • Marketing: these help measure our own marketing, for example which of our emails led to a visit or a sale.

Shopify lists each cookie, what it does and how long it lasts at shopify.com/legal/cookies. Shopify's own store cookies last up to one year.

How we protect it

  • Our site and checkout run on Shopify and use an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
