   O. Reg. 17/05 s. 18, as quoted in search, says ss. 22, 23 and 44 to 47 of the Act don't apply to an internet agreement that is also a future performance agreement. So the right reaches internet orders if late delivery is s. 26, but not if it's s. 23, as `requirements.md` ON-15 guessed. Please confirm:
