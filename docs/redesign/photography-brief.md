# Photography brief: Parkdale Brass jewelry launch

For the photographer and stylist. It replaces every placeholder in the design concepts. Questions go to the Parkdale Brass owner.

## The look in one line

**Real brass, true colour, daylight, quiet surfaces.** It should feel like a well-made object on a workbench in a good menswear shop: warm and tactile, never glossy, glamorous or "luxury gold".

- **Light:** directional natural or daylight-balanced light from one side, with soft falloff and real but gentle shadows. No ring lights, no hard flash, no coloured gels.
- **Surfaces and props:** linen (oatmeal, undyed), honed stone, aged wood, plain kraft card. Workwear fabrics for on-body shots: raw or washed denim, wool, waxed cotton, plain oxford shirting. Props should be sparse and not branded.
- **Palette:** warm neutrals that sit with the site: ivory `#F5F1E8`, stone `#E4DACA`, charcoal `#242420`, leather browns. Avoid saturated colour, pure white sweeps and black velvet.
- **Models:** adults of varied age, gender and skin tone. Hands and wrists with natural, clean nails. Workwear and heritage styling, not fashion editorial.
- **Feel:** honest and close-up. Show texture, edges and how a piece sits on the body.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Colour accuracy over mood.** The brass in the photo must match the piece in the hand. Shoot a colour checker or grey card in each lighting setup, and colour-correct to it.
2. **No AI-generated, composited or re-coloured product images.** Don't change a piece's colour, finish or shape in retouching. Dust, lint and fingerprint removal is fine; removing real tooling marks or patina is not, unless the product ships without them.
3. **Show the finish as sold.** Photograph each finish separately. If a finish patinas, mark any "aged" shot as such (the file name gets `-aged`) so it's never used as the default image.
4. **No text, logos or watermarks in any image.** The site adds text in HTML.
5. **Model and property releases** for every recognisable person and location, kept by the owner.

## Shot list

### Per product, per finish (4 shots, required)

| # | Shot | Ratio / min size | Direction | Used on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | **Front / ¾ view** | 4:5, 2048×2560 | Centred, about 60% of the frame, on a stone or linen ground. Consistent camera height and ground across the whole range. | Card image, PDP first image |
| P2 | **Reverse / opening / closure** | 4:5, 2048×2560 | Same set as P1. Shows the cuff opening, clasp, bail or signet underside. | Card hover image, PDP |
| P3 | **Macro of the finish** | 4:5, 2048×2560 | Close enough to read grain, edge radius and any stamp. Raking light. | PDP |
| P4 | **On body, for scale** | 4:5, 2048×2560 | Wrist, neck or hand in neutral workwear, framed so size is clear. Natural pose. | PDP |

Optional: P5 packaging (only if it's the real packaging that ships), P6 stacked or paired with another launch piece.

**Consistency:** P1 and P2 must match across every product (same ground, light, camera angle, crop), so the collection grid reads as one set. Lock the set and keep a reference frame.

## Technical delivery

- **Format:** JPG, quality 85–90, **sRGB**, 8-bit. Also keep the RAW / layered masters.
- **Sizes:** at least the minimums above. Shopify makes the smaller versions.
- **Crops:** deliver each listed crop as its own file; don't rely on automatic cropping. Mark the focal point in the file list.
- **Retouching:** clean and natural, per the non-negotiables. Match exposure and white balance across each set.

## File naming

`{product-handle}-{finish}-{shot}-{nn}.jpg` for product shots, `pb-{shot-id}-{desc}-{crop}.jpg` for editorial. All lowercase, hyphens only.

```text
the-junction-brushed-p1-01.jpg
the-junction-brushed-p3-01.jpg
the-junction-polished-p4-01.jpg
the-junction-brushed-p3-aged-01.jpg
pb-e1-hero-desktop.jpg
pb-e1-hero-mobile.jpg
pb-e1-hero-og.jpg
pb-e4-lifestyle-desktop.jpg
```

Deliver one folder per product, plus an `editorial/` folder, with a short spreadsheet: file name, product, finish, shot, and a **suggested alt text**. Alt text pattern: "{Product} {type} in {finish} brass, {view}". Example: "The Junction open cuff in brushed brass, worn on wrist".

## Usage and rights

Confirm in writing before the shoot: usage across the website, social, email and ads, its duration and territory, and any model usage limits. The owner keeps copies of all releases.
