Who We Are

Parkdale Brass provides high-quality, sleek, and 20th century inspired smoking pipes that are rugged and can withstand the ups and downs of day-to-day life. Driven by the desire to pay homage to the style and quality of smoke wear of the past, we create pipes with attention to detail and subtle sophistication. Our designs combine quality and durability, offering long-lasting cannabis accessories that are timeless With brass as our main ingredient, we create resilient and durable accessories that are perfect for your real-life smoke sessions wherever they may be.

What We Do

At Parkdale Brass, we pride ourselves on producing metal smoking pipes that uphold our ideals of quality and craftsmanship. With our accessories, you can always count on:

  • High-Quality Smokeware: Design and craftsmanship that won’t let you down. We strive to provide high-quality metal smoking pipes that deliver an elegant and unique experience unlike any other you’ve had before.
  • Medical-Grade Brass: Don’t let the shine fool you, our chrome-plated brass is also extremely durable and can stand the test of time without breaking or shattering. Also, the plating makes this metal completely safe to use and non-toxic, as well. 
  • Functionality: The Brockton Mk2 features a modular body that is completely deconstructible for cleaning and also for attaching to future models. Furthermore, it has a magnetic lid that seals to protect your flower and a large bowl that lights from the bottom saving your finger (and hair) from being burnt.
  • Upgraded Smoke Sessions: You deserve products that match your lifestyle. We create exceptional smokeware with innovative design and undeniable style. Our products breathe life back into the ritual of smoking. Parkdale Brass fuses functionality and elegance to create exceptional cannabis accessories for those looking for luxurious smoke.
