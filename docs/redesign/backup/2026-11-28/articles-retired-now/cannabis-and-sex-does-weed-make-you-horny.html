Some individuals report feeling an increased sense of sexual arousal, or horniness, after consuming cannabis. The Science Behind Cannabis and Sexual Arousal

Research indicates that the use of cannabis and sexual arousal may be linked. The endocannabinoid system, a complex system of neurotransmitters and receptors in our bodies, plays a role in regulating sexual function. THC, the psychoactive compound found in cannabis, can interact with these receptors and potentially enhance sexual function.

Studies have shown that THC can increase levels of dopamine, a neurotransmitter associated with pleasure and reward. This increase in dopamine can lead to enhanced sexual desire and arousal. THC can also decrease anxiety, which can be a factor in inhibiting sexual desire.

However, it's important to note that the effects of cannabis on sexual arousal are not fully understood and can vary widely from person to person. The effects can depend on factors such as individual biology, strain of cannabis, and methods of consumption.
